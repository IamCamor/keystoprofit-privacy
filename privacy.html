KeysToProfit Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

KeysToProfit (“the App”) is a macOS productivity utility that lets you create global keyboard shortcuts to paste text, open applications, and open websites.

Data we collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. Shortcuts and settings are stored locally on your Mac using standard system storage (UserDefaults). There is no account system and no analytics SDK.

Permissions

The App may request Accessibility permission so it can paste text into the frontmost application when you press a shortcut you configured. This permission is used only for that purpose on your device.

Third parties

The App does not share data with third parties. Distribution through the Mac App Store is subject to Apple’s privacy terms.

Children

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect children’s data.
